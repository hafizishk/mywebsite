Privacy in the SPL app

The Singapore Premier League fan app, built by Stackform Studios. Last updated 6 November 2026.

What the app collects

  • How the app is used, without your name. Which screens open, what is tapped, which sponsor sections are seen and which alerts arrive, each with a random code for the phone. It tells the league which features fans use and how often partners are seen. It is never linked to your name, email or phone number.
  • Your votes. When you vote for Player or Goal of the Month, the app signs the phone in with an anonymous login so each fan votes once a month. The league sees the totals, not who voted for whom.
  • Your choices on the phone. The club you follow, your alert settings, your look (dark or light) and your name if you type one in, stored on your phone only.

What it does not collect

No contacts, photos, location, microphone or advertising ID. Nothing is sold.

Where it is kept

On a database in Singapore (Supabase, Southeast Asia region), run for the league. Usage records are kept for up to 24 months, votes for the season they belong to.

Alerts

If you allow notifications, the app sends goals, kick-offs, results and news for the clubs and competitions you choose. Change or turn them off in Settings in the app or in your phone's settings.

Your rights

Under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act you can ask what is held about you and ask for it to be deleted. Deleting the app removes everything stored on the phone. To delete your votes and usage records, write to the address below with the date you installed the app.
